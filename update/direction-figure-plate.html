Microglomeruli Segmentation

Instance segmentation of synaptic boutons in Drosophila brain microscopy, and the desktop tool that puts it in the lab.

Boutons sit packed against each other in the calyx, so a semantic model would paint one confluent blob across a dozen of them and score well on pixel overlap while answering the wrong question. The biological question is how many there are and how big each one is. That makes the interesting failure mode a merge, not a miss, and it makes evaluation a matter of instance matching in physical units rather than voxel counting.

Dataset
Hand-annotated from scratch in napari. No public benchmark exists at this acquisition setting.
Compared
MicroSAM Large & Base, vs. CellposeSAM, Swin UNETR, nnU-Net, and the lab's semi-manual Imaris workflow
Shipped as
BoutonViewer, a napari app. Auto-derives voxel calibration per acquisition type, caches inference so a display change doesn't re-run the model, reports volume & area in µm³ / µm².
Recall is the headline: a missed bouton is a permanent counting error, a false positive is filterable. Test set: 2 specimens, 61 ground-truth instances.

TechDrishti

A fully autonomous Hindi tech-news publication. It has been publishing every morning without me.

Cheap-model triage filters non-news before the expensive model ever sees it, which is the whole reason the economics work. Reasoning and output share one token budget on these models, so when reasoning ran long the API returned nothing at all — planning and writing had to be split across calls.

Deep Research Agent

An autonomous research loop where grounding is enforced in code, not requested in a prompt.

Two rounds of increasingly explicit “don’t hallucinate” prompting failed reproducibly, so the check moved into code. Then I measured integrating it back into TechDrishti: roughly +81 minutes onto a 9–20 minute run, and about $0.01 to $0.17 per run. I left it out.

That row is marked Not shipped on purpose. Measuring something and deciding against it is a result.
